# Reta Marketing — TikTok Distribution Pack

Free, faceless TikTok content kit for the Reta dating affiliate hub. 12 ready-to-record scripts plus the bio + hook framework to keep you posting consistently for the first month.

## What's in here

- **`tiktok-bio-and-strategy.md`** — bio copy, link-in-bio strategy, account hygiene rules, 30-day posting schedule.
- **`tiktok-hooks-and-templates.md`** — 8 hook formulas you can mix and match for any new topic, plus the caption template.
- **`tiktok-scripts-mature.md`** — 4 full scripts targeting the 40+/50+/60+ audience.
- **`tiktok-scripts-bbw.md`** — 4 full scripts for body-positive singles.
- **`tiktok-scripts-regional.md`** — 4 full scripts for local/regional dating.

## How to use a script

Each script gives you:

1. **3 hook variants** — record all three; the algorithm tells you which works.
2. **Full voiceover read** — paste into ElevenLabs (free tier) or use TikTok's text-to-speech.
3. **Visual direction** — what b-roll or text-on-screen to show, second by second.
4. **Caption + hashtags** — copy-paste ready.

## Tools you need (all free)

- **TikTok app** (record, captions, scheduling).
- **CapCut** (free) — for trims, cuts, beat sync.
- **Pexels / Pixabay** — license-free b-roll (search "couple cafe", "city street", "phone notification", etc.).
- **ElevenLabs free tier** OR TikTok built-in TTS — for voiceover if you don't want to use your own voice.
- **Canva free** — for any text-on-screen graphics.

You do NOT need a camera, a microphone, or to show your face.

## Important rules

These are non-negotiable. They keep your account alive and your Adsterra CPA payouts intact.

1. **No claims of guaranteed dates, matches, or romance.** Talk about platforms, habits, and ideas.
2. **No fake testimonials.** No "Sarah, 52, met her husband on..." — you do not know her, and you cannot fake her.
3. **No before/after, no body close-ups, no thirst traps.** Aesthetic = warm, calm, magazine-y.
4. **No DMing strangers** to drop your link. The link goes in your bio, full stop.
5. **No commenting your link** under other people's videos. Same rule.
6. **18+ in your bio.** Mandatory.
