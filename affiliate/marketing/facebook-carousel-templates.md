# Facebook Pro — Carousel Post Templates

Carousels (multi-image posts) on Facebook Pro outperform single-image posts by 4-5x because they force horizontal swipe = more time on post = more reach.

3 ready-to-build carousels, one per niche. Each turns a blog article into 8 swipeable slides.

**Format for all:** 1080x1080 (square). Built free in Canva > "Instagram Post" template (same dimensions work on FB).

**Visual style across all carousels:**
- Background: cream (`#fdf5f9`) or dark navy (`#1a1020`)
- Body text: serif headers (DM Serif Display), sans body (DM Sans)
- Accent color: pink `#e8317a`
- No stock photos of people. Use light-and-shadow stills, hands, fabric, light through windows.

## Production tips

### Build once, use on 3 platforms

A 1080x1080 carousel built in Canva exports to:
- **Facebook** — original size, swipe carousel
- **Instagram** — same file, same format
- **LinkedIn** (if you ever go there) — works as a document carousel

Build once, post three times.

### What NOT to put on a slide

- Specific dating-platform brand names. Send to Reta for the recommendation.
- "Click the link" — the link goes in the caption + bio, never on the image.
- Real screenshots of dating apps. Mock them up in Canva instead.
- Photos of identifiable people (legal risk, plus FB downranks unverified faces).

### Iteration rhythm

Post a carousel. Wait 7 days. Look at:

- **Reach:** is FB pushing it? (>1k for new accounts is a green light)
- **Saves:** highest-value engagement metric
- **Comments:** are people engaging with the topic?

Whichever niche carousel performs best, build the next 3 carousels in that niche before going wide again.
