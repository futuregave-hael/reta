# Facebook Pro Mode — Strategy Pack

Facebook's Professional Mode lets a personal profile monetize like a creator page (Reels Play, Stars, branded content) without losing personal-profile reach. For Reta, FB Pro is a **second-channel** that works especially well for the mature dating niche.

This doc covers: when FB Pro beats TikTok, the setup, the content plan (you can reuse the TikTok scripts), the compliance rules, and the 30-day schedule.

## When FB Pro is the right channel

| Niche | TikTok | FB Pro | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mature 40+/50+/60+** | OK | **Best** | FB's core audience is 35-65. Mature dating CTR on FB Reels is 2-3x TikTok. |
| **Body-positive** | **Best** | Good | TikTok's algorithm rewards this niche harder. FB is supplemental. |
| **Local / regional** | Good | **Best** | FB groups + neighbourhood pages give you city-level reach TikTok can't match. |

If your time is limited, **post mature-niche content first to FB Reels**, body-positive first to TikTok.

## What FB Pro can and can't do

### Can
- Run **Reels Play Bonus** — Meta pays you per 1,000 qualifying Reel plays, separate from affiliate.
- Add **one external bio link** (Reta homepage).
- Create a **Featured Link** under your name.
- Get **Stars** from viewers (small but real income).
- Cross-post Reels to Instagram automatically.

### Can't
- Post affiliate or shortened links in Reel captions or posts. Meta classifies these as spam and suppresses reach by 70-90%.
- Promote "hookup" or sexually explicit dating apps. Account-level ban risk.
- Run paid ads to direct affiliate URLs without a verified business + landing page (use Reta as the landing).
- Mass-post in dating groups. 24-hour ban on first detection.

## Account setup (45 minutes)

### Step 1 — Switch on Pro Mode

1. Go to your personal Facebook profile.
2. Tap your profile photo, scroll to "Switch to Professional Mode" (or visit `facebook.com/professional_mode`).
3. Pick the category **"Digital creator"** (not "Public figure," not "Blogger" — those have stricter ad policies).

### Step 2 — Profile copy (3 versions)

Mirror your TikTok bio. Pick the version that matches your primary niche.

**Mature-leaning:**
```
Honest dating reads for grown-ups. 40+, body-positive, local.
18+ · Articles, voice notes, no cringe.
```

**Body-positive-leaning:**
```
Body-positive dating. No cringe, no diet talk.
18+ · Full reads in the link below.
```

**Mixed:**
```
Dating, told straight. Every age, every shape, every city.
18+
```

### Step 3 — Bio link

Set the **single Featured Link** to `https://reta.pages.dev` (or your custom domain). Title: "Read Reta — full guides."

Don't link `linktr.ee`, `bit.ly`, or anything that looks like a redirect. Meta's classifier flags those.

### Step 4 — Profile photo and cover

- **Profile photo:** Same as TikTok — abstract logo only. The "R" mark from `affiliate/public/favicon.svg` works.
- **Cover photo:** A simple text-on-photo image with your tagline. Canva > "Facebook Cover" template. Avoid stock-people photos.

### Step 5 — Account warm-up (5 days)

Same logic as TikTok warm-up. Don't post anything affiliate-adjacent for the first 5 days:

- Day 1-3: like, share, and comment on 3-5 popular Reels in the dating-advice niche each day.
- Day 4: post one **non-affiliate** Reel — a generic reflection on dating, no link.
- Day 5: post one more, plus one carousel post.
- Day 6: now you can switch to scheduled niche content.

Skipping the warm-up is the #1 reason new pro accounts get auto-flagged.

## Content strategy

### Reels (primary format)

**Reuse the 12 TikTok scripts directly.** The scripts in `tiktok-scripts-mature.md`, `tiktok-scripts-bbw.md`, and `tiktok-scripts-regional.md` are already 9:16, 22-45 seconds, no-face, and policy-safe. They drop straight into FB Reels.

Only changes per script for FB:

| TikTok line | Replace with on FB |
|---|---|
| "Link in bio" | "Read more — link on my profile" |
| "Save this" | "Tap save on the corner" (FB users save less; lower the ask) |
| Hashtags (7) | 3-4 hashtags max — FB rewards fewer tags than TikTok |
| Top-of-Reel watermark | None — strip the TikTok watermark with Snaptik or CapCut export |

### Carousel posts (secondary format)

Carousels (multi-image posts) outperform single images on FB Pro by 4-5x. Convert each blog article into one carousel:

- **Slide 1:** Hook (the article title, large text on cream background)
- **Slides 2-7:** One key idea per slide, 30 words max
- **Slide 8:** "Full guide on Reta — link on my profile"

Use Canva's "Instagram Carousel" template (1080x1080) — same dimensions work on FB.

See `facebook-carousel-templates.md` for 3 ready-to-build carousels (one per niche).

### Long-form posts (tertiary)

Write a 200-300 word version of the blog article directly in a FB post. Add at the bottom:

```
Full guide (with bonus tips) > on my profile link
```

These get less reach than Reels but they convert harder for the audience that does see them. Aim for 1 long post per week.

## Reels Play Bonus (extra cash)

Meta pays per qualifying play. Rates fluctuate but typical for new accounts in 2026:

| Plays | Estimated payout |
|---|---|
| 100k | $20-50 |
| 1M | $200-500 |
| 10M | $2,000-5,000 |

To qualify:
- Minimum 5 published Reels.
- 1,000 followers, 60 days old account.
- Original audio (use TikTok's TTS or a free ElevenLabs voiceover, not copyrighted music).
- Vertical video, 60 seconds or less.

This is your "while you wait for CPA conversions" income. Treat it as bonus, not core.

## FB Groups (organic, slow, powerful)

Done right, FB groups are the highest-converting traffic source for mature dating. Done wrong, they get you banned in a day.

### The right way

1. Join 5-10 groups in your niche. Examples: "Dating After 50 Support," "Single Again at 60," "Body Positive Singles," "[Your City] Singles Mingle."
2. **Read the rules.** Most ban affiliate links and self-promo outright.
3. Spend the first 30 days **only commenting** on other people's posts. Be useful, not promotional.
4. After 30 days, post **one personal-experience post per group per month**. No link in the post itself. If someone asks "where did you read that?" in comments, reply with: "I write about this on my profile — link in bio."

### The wrong way (instant ban)

- Joining a group and posting a link day one.
- Posting the same content across multiple groups in the same day.
- DMing group members.
- Using a "promo post" link in the comments.
- Tagging strangers.

### Where to post YOUR OWN posts

Don't make a Page yet. Pages get less organic reach than Pro profiles. Post to your **profile** with public visibility. Pages only make sense once you're running paid ads (which we're not, see "What we don't do" below).

Use FB's built-in scheduler (Creator Studio > Composer > "Schedule"). Don't use third-party tools — they get downweighted.

## Compliance — what's different from TikTok

Meta is stricter than TikTok on three things. Watch these.

### 1. External links in post body

Don't paste affiliate URLs, `/go/<offer>` URLs, or short links anywhere except your bio. Meta's spam classifier autodetects affiliate URLs and silently caps reach.

If you absolutely must mention a URL in a comment, use the FULL canonical Reta URL (e.g., `https://reta.pages.dev/blog/dating-after-50-online-guide/`) — never a redirect.

### 2. "Branded content" disclosure

If you mention a specific dating platform by name in a Reel or post, tag it as "Paid partnership" via FB's branded-content tool. The Reta site itself handles the legal disclosure (see `/disclaimer/`); for FB you must do the platform-level tag too if you ever name an offer directly.

The cleaner path: **don't name specific dating apps in your content.** Talk about categories ("body-positive dating apps," "local-first dating apps"). Send people to Reta to see the named recommendation.

### 3. Misleading claims

Meta's "deceptive practices" policy is broader than TikTok's. Avoid:

- Fake testimonials or "I met my partner on..." stories you didn't live.
- Numerical claims ("get 5x more matches") without a real source.
- Fake urgency ("only 24 hours left").
- Cropping a real screenshot to imply a different message.

## Monetization stack on FB Pro

This is the layered income model. Don't depend on one stream.

```
1. Reels Play Bonus       (Meta pays you per 1k plays)         <- 0-90 days
2. Stars from viewers     (small, ambient)                     <- 30+ days
3. Adsterra CPA via Reta  (your main income)                   <- 60-180 days
4. Branded partnerships   (real platforms paying for posts)    <- 6+ months
```

Stream 1-2 are FB's payouts. Stream 3 is your reason for being there. Stream 4 is the long game — once you have 5k followers, dating platforms will sometimes DM you for direct sponsorships at higher rates than CPA.

## What we DON'T do on FB Pro

To be clear, since you've heard pitches for these elsewhere:

1. **No paid FB Ads to direct CPA links.** Meta requires a verified business, a clear landing page, and dating ads have a special vertical-approval process. We bypass all that by sending paid traffic (when you're ready) to **Reta articles**, not offers.
2. **No buying followers.** Meta detects fake followers in days. Account dies.
3. **No mass DM bots.** Even one-day campaigns of automated DMs get accounts permanently banned.
4. **No "engagement pods."** They worked in 2018, they kill reach in 2026.
5. **No fake "I just tried this app" videos.** Even if you actually did try it, framing it as discovery when you're affiliated is deceptive.

## When you've got 1,000 followers

Three upgrades unlock:

1. **Apply for Reels Play Bonus** if you haven't already (Meta Business Suite > Monetization).
2. **Start a small FB Group** of your own. "Reta Reads — Dating for Grown-Ups" or similar. Post a weekly thread. Members are 5-10x more loyal than feed followers.
3. **Add Stars to your Reels** (the donate button). Costs nothing.
