# TikTok Bio + Account Strategy

## Account name

Pick one of the three patterns. Keep it neutral — TikTok shadow-bans accounts that read as overtly affiliate.

- **`@reta.reads`** — looks like a publication, low risk.
- **`@datingdesk`** — generic editorial vibe.
- **`@dating.honestly`** — alt option if the above are taken.

Avoid: "deals", "free", "hookup", "match" — these flag dating-spam classifiers.

## Bio (copy-paste)

Pick the version that matches your niche focus. Each is under TikTok's 80-character limit.

### Mature-leaning bio
```
Honest dating reads for grown-ups. 40+, body-positive, local.
18+ · link below
```

### Body-positive-leaning bio
```
Body-positive dating advice. No cringe. No diet talk.
18+ · full reads below
```

### Mixed-niche bio
```
Dating, told straight.
Every age, every shape, every city. 18+
```

## Profile photo

A clean, abstract logo. Use the favicon from `affiliate/public/favicon.svg` rendered at 200x200 — dark background with the cursive "R" in pink. Do **not** use a stock person photo (it triggers "fake influencer" flags).

## Link in bio

Single link to `https://reta.pages.dev` (or your custom domain).
The homepage already functions as a link-in-bio with the three offer cards above the fold. You don't need Linktree, Beacons, or any third-party redirector.

## What to pin (3 slots)

After you have 5+ videos, pin these three:

1. Your **best-performing hook** so far (TikTok rewards pinned-engagement consistency).
2. One video from your **strongest niche** (whichever vertical is converting clicks).
3. A **series-starter** ("day 1 of 30") that drives follows.

## Account warm-up (days 1 to 7)

Do this before publishing anything you care about:

- Day 1: open the account, add bio + photo + link. Don't post.
- Day 2: scroll the For You page for 30 minutes. Like 10 videos in your niche. Comment thoughtfully on 3.
- Day 3: same. Follow 5 accounts in your niche.
- Day 4: post your **first** video (a low-stakes one — `regional-1` is good).
- Day 5 to 7: scroll, like, comment. Don't post.
- Day 8: post #2.

This pattern signals "human user" before the algorithm sees affiliate-like behaviour.

## Account hygiene rules

These keep you out of the spam ghetto.

1. **One niche per video.** Don't mix mature + BBW in the same script.
2. **Native uploads only.** No watermarks from other platforms (no Reels watermark, no YouTube Shorts watermark).
3. **No reply-bait.** Don't say "comment YES if..."
4. **Captions on screen.** Even if you use voiceover. 80% of TikTok plays without sound.
5. **3-second rewatch test.** If your hook doesn't make you want to keep watching, re-shoot.
6. **No editing software watermarks.** CapCut adds one by default — turn it off in settings.
7. **Don't delete underperformers.** Wait 7 days. Some videos pop late.
8. **Don't comment your link** anywhere. Drives shadow-bans within hours.

## Repurposing flowchart

After you've posted 12 videos to TikTok, do this:

```
TikTok video (native)
  ├── re-upload to Instagram Reels (no edit, just re-encode through CapCut)
  ├── re-upload to YouTube Shorts (same)
  └── pull the voiceover, add Pinterest-style image, post as Pinterest pin
```

Same content, 4x distribution. Each platform sees it as native.

## What to do when a video flops

Don't delete. After 72 hours of <500 views:

1. Repost the same script with **a different hook**. Save as a separate video.
2. If the second post also flops, archive the topic for a month.
3. The algorithm rewards persistence. 30% of viral videos for new accounts come from re-shoots.
