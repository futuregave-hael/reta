## Script 3 — "Message a local like a local"

- **Length:** 30 seconds
- **Goal:** saves + bio clicks
- **Linked article:** `local-dating-meet-people-your-city`

### Hook variants

1. "Message a local like a local. The first line that always gets a reply."
2. "Why 'hi gorgeous' fails and 'best dosa in town?' works."
3. "The local-anchored opening message that beats every compliment."

### Voiceover (28 seconds)

> Locals match harder when you talk like a local.
>
> "Hi gorgeous" is generic. It could be sent to anyone, in any city, on any app. The brain registers it as low effort.
>
> Try a local anchor instead. "You said you grew up here. Best dosa in town?"
>
> Or: "That photo at the riverside, is that the bridge near the old market?"
>
> You're not flirting yet. You're proving you read their profile and you live in the same world they do.
>
> One specific question about a place beats ten compliments. Try it for a week.
>
