# TikTok Hooks + Caption Templates

The first 1.5 seconds decide whether a video gets to 100, 1,000, or 100,000 views. Below are 8 hook formulas that work for the dating niche without being clickbait.

## The 8 hook formulas

### 1. The contrarian
> "Stop [common advice]. Here's what actually works."

Examples:
- "Stop putting 'sapiosexual' in your bio. Here's what actually works."
- "Stop swiping globally if you want a coffee on Saturday."
- "Stop hiding your full body in dating photos. Here's why."

### 2. The number drop
> "[Number] things nobody tells you about [topic]."

Examples:
- "3 things nobody tells you about dating after 50."
- "5 red flags I learned to spot the hard way."
- "7 dating-app features that filter out catfish."

### 3. The mistake confession
> "I used to [mistake]. Here's what changed."

Examples:
- "I used to message 'hey beautiful'. Here's what works instead."
- "I used to swipe at 2am. Here's why it killed my matches."

### 4. The hard rule
> "If [signal], they're not [thing]. Hard rule."

Examples:
- "If they won't video call, they're not real. Hard rule."
- "If your photos are 5 years old, you're not dating yourself. Hard rule."

### 5. The audience callout
> "If you're [demographic], this is for you."

Examples:
- "If you're 50+ and just getting back into dating, watch this."
- "If you've been ghosted in the last week, this is for you."
- "If you're plus-size and tired of swiping, this is for you."

### 6. The question
> "What's the [thing] that actually [outcome]?"

Examples:
- "What's the bio line that filters out 80% of bad matches?"
- "What's the first message that gets a reply in your 50s?"

### 7. The category-creator
> "There's a name for the [thing] you've been doing."

Examples:
- "There's a name for the dating burnout you're feeling."
- "There's a word for the 'audition' you do in your photos."

### 8. The story open
> "Last [time period], [event]. Here's what I learned."

Examples:
- "Last month I deleted three dating apps. Here's what happened."
- "Last year I went on 8 first dates. Here's the only pattern that mattered."

## Caption template

Write captions to add value, not to rehash the video. They appear under the video and are searchable.

```
[1-line summary of the takeaway, no question marks]

Read the full guide, link in bio

[3 hashtags from the high-volume tier]
[3 hashtags from the niche tier]
[1 super-niche tag]
```

### Example caption (for a "dating after 50" video)

```
The voice-note era changed dating in your 50s. Tone matters more than vocabulary.

Read the full guide, link in bio

#datingafter50 #datinglife #onlinedating
#maturedating #dating50plus #datingadvice
#voicenoteera
```

## Hashtag strategy

Mix three tiers in every post. Aim for 7 tags total.

### High-volume (3 tags)

These are big enough to fish for new viewers, small enough that you're not invisible.

- `#datingadvice` (3.5B+ views)
- `#datinglife` (3B+)
- `#onlinedating` (2B+)
- `#datingtips` (5B+)
- `#relationshipadvice` (8B+)

### Niche (3 tags)

These are where your community actually lives.

**Mature:**
- `#datingafter50` `#datingafter40` `#datingafter60` `#dating50plus` `#maturedating`

**Body-positive:**
- `#bodypositivity` `#plussizedating` `#bbwdating` `#bodyconfidence` `#plussizelife`

**Regional:**
- `#localdating` `#datinginthecity` `#singleinthecity` `#datingapps`

### Super-niche (1 tag)

Tiny tag that signals you're in-community. Pick from:

- `#voicenoteera` `#datingreset` `#datingoverit` `#bodypositivedating` `#singleat50`

Don't use trending audio that's NSFW or politically loaded. The algorithm penalises mismatches.

## Audio strategy

For faceless content, the audio carries the vibe.

| Mood | Use this |
|---|---|
| Reflective / mature | Soft piano, slow indie acoustic, lo-fi |
| Body-positive | Empowering pop instrumental (no lyrics if you have voiceover) |
| Local / regional | City ambient, jazz cafe, walking sound |
| Tip / list video | Trending TikTok beat at 30% volume under voiceover |

Always use TikTok's native sound library. Using copyrighted music caps your reach.

## Length rules

- **Hooks tested under 15 seconds:** these are your "free attention" videos. Use them to test new ideas.
- **30 to 45 seconds:** the workhorse length. Most of these scripts are this size.
- **60 to 90 seconds:** only when you have a story or a series-opener. Watch-through % drops fast above 60s.
- **Above 90 seconds:** avoid for the first 90 days. Algorithm penalises low completion rate.
