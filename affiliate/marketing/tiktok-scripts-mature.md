- **Length:** 35 seconds
- **Goal:** profile follow + bio link click

2. "If you're 50 and just got back into dating, this is the part nobody warns you about."

> Two. The general apps are not built for you. The audience that fits you is on smaller, age-matched platforms. Five thousand people who get it beats five million who don't.
>
> Three. Slow is fine. Nobody is racing you. The best first dates I've heard about took two weeks to set up, not two days.
>
> Full guide is in the bio. Take what you need.

- **Length:** 45 seconds (story-format, longer is OK)

- **Linked article:** `dating-after-50-online-guide` + `dating-after-divorce-50` (when published)

1. "Dating after divorce at 51, day one. Here's what nobody told me."
2. "Three things I learned in the first week of dating again at 51."
3. "I just downloaded a dating app for the first time in 23 years. Here's day one."

### Voiceover (42 seconds, slower cadence)

> Day one of dating after divorce at 51.
>
> You don't need to find the love of your life today. You don't need to find one this month.

>
> The whole thing is supposed to feel like learning a new language, not like a job interview. Slow. Mostly mistakes. Some surprise wins.
>
