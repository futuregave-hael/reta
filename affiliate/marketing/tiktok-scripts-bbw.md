- **Length:** 35 seconds

- **Linked article:** `body-confidence-dating-tips`

### Hook variants

1. "The dating-photo advice that wasn't written for us. Here's what actually works."
2. "If you're plus-size, the standard photo advice is sabotaging your matches."
3. "Three dating-photo rules I'm rewriting because they were never written for plus-size singles."

### Voiceover (32 seconds)

> Most dating photo advice was written for one body type. Ignore it.
>
> Here's what actually works.
>
> One. Use one full-body photo. In good light, in a setting you love. Hiding your body makes the meet awkward. Owning it makes it easy.
>
> Two. One photo of you laughing. Not posed. Catch yourself mid-life.
>
> Three. One photo of you doing something. Cooking, reading, holding your dog. Verbs are more attractive than nouns.
>
> The point isn't to look like someone else. It's to look enough like yourself that someone can recognise you on a coffee date.
>

2. "The grown-up reply to a fat-shaming DM. No drama, no apology."
3. "Save this for the next time someone is rude to you on a dating app."

### Voiceover (28 seconds)

> Three replies to a fat-shaming dating app message, ranked.
>
> Option three: a long, defensive paragraph explaining why they're wrong. Don't. They didn't earn it.
>

>
> Save this. Use it the next time. You don't owe anyone a debate about your worth.

## Script 4 — "Confidence is a habit, not a personality trait"

- **Length:** 40 seconds (series-opener — "day 1 of 7")
- **Goal:** follows
- **Linked article:** `body-confidence-dating-tips`

### Hook variants

1. "Confidence is a habit, not a personality trait. Day 1 of 7."
2. "The seven-day confidence reset for plus-size dating. Day one."
3. "If 'be confident' has never worked for you, try this seven-day version instead."

### Voiceover (38 seconds)

> "Be confident" is the worst dating advice because it skips a step.
>
> You're supposed to become confident before you can be confident, and that gap is where most of us get stuck.
>
> Confidence is a habit stack. So this is a seven-day version.
>
> Day one. Today's only job: catch yourself in the mirror once and don't critique. Just notice. That's it.
>
> Don't dress up. Don't do anything different. Walk past, glance, move on.
>
> Most of us have practiced the critique loop for so long it's automatic. Day one is the interruption.
>
> The dating app, the messages, the matches, all of that comes later. Day one is just here.
>
> Follow for day two tomorrow. Full reset guide in the bio.
