        ↑                          ↑

Each article has a `featuredOffer` (already in front-matter). Pick the offer that best matches the article's intent.

### Production rules

- 25–45 seconds per video.
- Hook in the first 1.5 seconds.
- One idea per video. If you have two, that is two videos.
- Captions burned-in. (TikTok's built-in caption tool is fine.)
- End-card: "Full read at the link in bio."
- Bio link: `https://reta.pages.dev` (homepage acts as link-in-bio).

### Cadence

- 3 TikToks per week, mixed categories.
- Pin the best three to your profile.
- Repurpose to **YouTube Shorts** and **Instagram Reels** with no edit.

## Pinterest (bonus)

- One pin per article. 1000x1500 (vertical), text on top of soft image.
- Pin URL: **the blog article**, never `/go/<offer>`.
- Group boards: search "dating advice," "relationship tips 40s/50s," "body positive."
- Cadence: 5 pins per day across 1 article (different captions, different pin images). Use a free Pinterest scheduler (Tailwind has a free trial, or just batch-schedule with Pinterest's built-in tool).

## When you have $50/month spare

Reinvest into one of these — never paid ads to direct CPA links:

1. **Custom domain** (~$10/year): replaces `*.pages.dev`, removes the affiliate-friendliness penalty on Pinterest and email tools.
2. **Plausible Analytics** ($9/month): cookie-free analytics, lets you optimise traffic sources.
3. **A second writer** ($30/article on Upwork) to double publishing speed.

Skip paid ads to direct offers until you have at least 3 months of organic data showing which offers convert.
