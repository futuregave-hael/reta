Most popular dating apps are global products with a "distance" filter on top. The result: you scroll past 200 profiles and three of them live within driving distance, and two of those last logged in eight months ago.

If your goal is "meet someone for coffee this week," the algorithm is working against you. Here is the fix.

## Why "global app, local filter" fails

- **Cold inventory.** Inactive profiles bloat the results. You cannot tell the difference until you have wasted a message.
- **Visibility lottery.** Global apps boost photogenic outliers. A perfectly nice neighbour with one mediocre selfie is invisible.
- **Wrong density.** A city of half a million has plenty of local matches in real life. The app makes it feel like five.

## What to look for in a local-first platform

A platform built for local meetups behaves differently:

- **Active-in-last-7-days filter.** Non-negotiable.
- **City-level rooms or feeds.** Not just a radius slider.
- **Same-week meetup intent.** Some apps ask "When could you grab a coffee?" at signup. Match.
- **Neighbourhood granularity.** Even better when the app knows the difference between two neighbourhoods.

## The 30-minute rule

Set your search radius to "30 minutes by your usual transport." Not 50 km, not "open to long distance." If meeting them this week would take a flight, the conversation will eventually stall.

## Three small habits that help

### 1. Anchor messages in your city

"You said you grew up here — best dosa in town?" outperforms "You are gorgeous" by a wide margin. Locals match harder when you talk like a local.

### 2. Suggest a real, small first date

"Coffee at Filter on Saturday at 11" beats "we should hang out sometime." Specifics convert.

### 3. Reuse a venue you trust

Find one cafe, one walk loop, and one bar you genuinely like. Use them as your defaults. Decision fatigue kills first dates.

## A short list of red flags for "fake local" apps

- Profiles all 30+ km away on day one.
- "Premium" needed to see who liked you. (Common, but be skeptical of how aggressive the upsell is.)
- Same five profiles recycled.
- No "report" or "block" within two taps.
